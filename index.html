Project your pot to retirement. Historical return & volatility drive a range of outcomes (1,000+ simulations) — a spread of possibilities, not a promise.

Defaults come from your blended holdings (return & volatility) and latest recorded pot value — change any of them. Recorded total values · exact

Type in your pot's total value on the dates you have statements for. With two or more dated values the chart above shows your exact total value and growth — your real money, not an estimate from fund prices. (Today's value from the box at the top is included automatically.)

Contributions & withdrawals · optional

Record money you paid in (positive) or took out (negative) between your recorded values. This lets the chart separate true investment growth from the cash you added, and shows a money-weighted (IRR) return.

Record what funds you held and the dates you held them. Give each holding period a start and end date — leave the end blank to run it up to the latest prices (or the next period). We chain those daily-rebalanced periods into one continuous performance line.

Compare funds over time/ REBASED TO 100

Click funds to plot them together. Each line is rebased to 100 at the first date they all share, so funds with very different unit prices are directly comparable.

Lines show price (capital) return rebased to a common 100 — they exclude charges and any income/dividends. A line ending at 140 means +40% over the window shown.
