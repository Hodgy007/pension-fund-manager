🔒 Where your data lives: your holdings, £ amounts, pension statements, salary and date of birth stay on this device only — they are never uploaded, and there are no accounts or logins. Information tool, not financial advice. Figures are historical; past performance does not predict future returns. Ignores fees, charges, tax and how funds move together, and says nothing about your goals or timeframe. For a recommendation, speak to a regulated financial adviser.

Contributions & withdrawals · optional

Record money you paid in (positive) or took out (negative) between your recorded values. This lets the chart separate true investment growth from the cash you added, and shows a money-weighted (IRR) return.

Will it be enough?/ PROJECTION

Project your pot to retirement with UK tax built in (HMRC 2025/26, rest-of-UK). Historical return & volatility drive 1,500 simulations — a spread of possibilities, not a promise.

Defaults come from your blended holdings (return & volatility) and latest recorded pot. Shaded band = middle 80% of outcomes (10th–90th percentile); gold line = median. Tax relief is added to contributions and the retirement breakdown uses current income-tax bands, the 25% tax-free lump sum (capped at £268,275) and the £60,000 annual allowance. Estimates only — they ignore fund charges, the State Pension unless you add it, allowance taper/MPAA and your wider circumstances, and HMRC figures change each Budget. Not a forecast or financial advice.
